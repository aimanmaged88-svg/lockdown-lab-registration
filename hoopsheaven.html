House rules

Read it once. It matters. This is what keeps the runs good.

🏀Runs aren't confirmed or guaranteed. Hoops Heaven doesn't run the runs — players call them, players show up (or don't). We're just here to help the community get together and get runs in.
⚠️Play at your own risk. Public courts, real basketball. Look after your body, your stuff, your people and the court.
🌱The community IS the app. It grows on the people in it. Bring good energy, welcome new hoopers, leave the court better than you found it.
🧊Zero tolerance on bad behaviour. Disrespect, threats, abuse, discrimination — in the app or at a run — and you're gone. Banned indefinitely. One strike is enough.
🪪Real accounts only. Sign in with your Instagram — or a real email (Gmail, Apple ID, whatever) if you don't have IG. Burner and fake accounts get removed on sight.
🔨Bans stick. Break the rules and your handle, your email and your device are all blocked. A new account won't save you.
🧹We can remove any run, clip or account at any time to protect the community.
