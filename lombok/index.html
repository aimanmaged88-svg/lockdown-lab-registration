🏀 Jump Higher — 14-day plan

Two weeks to a bigger vertical: heavy lower-body strength + low-volume, high-intent plyos, 48 h between jump days, soft landings every rep. Gym days work with dumbbells/kettlebells or a proper rack; beach or grass is perfect for plyo days. Stop any rep that hurts a joint.
Measure: standing reach (flat feet, arm up) vs the highest point you touch on a max jump. The difference is your vertical. Same wall, same chalk, same shoes both times.
