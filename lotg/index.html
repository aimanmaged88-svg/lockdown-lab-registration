Who we are

Making a difference — on and off the court.

Love of the Game started in the gym and grew into a community. We shine a light on sport and the people in it — basketball first, but the NBL, the NRL and stories from right across NSW too. We talk honestly about men's mental health, run a school-based mentoring program, put on the events that bring people together, and back anyone trying to make a difference. Your journey, your story.

Schools program

The Next Play

Educator-developed · Built on the NSW PDHPE syllabus

A nine-week, school-based mentoring and behaviour-support program that takes Your Journey, Your Story off the court and into the classroom — built for the young people who need it most.

  • 9Weeks
  • 3Phases
  • NSW PDHPE syllabus
  • School-based
  • Mentoring + behaviour support
  1. 01 Weeks 1 – 3

    Understanding your journey

    Your goals, your ambitions, your plans — and who you are.

  2. 02 Weeks 4 – 6

    Creating your story

    Finding what needs to get done — and building the skills to do it.

  3. 03 Weeks 7 – 9

    Make it great

    Knowing that your next steps are truly yours.
