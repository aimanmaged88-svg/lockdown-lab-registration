FAQ + Security Card The 15 questions Yasser will ask. Say the shaded lines out loud in the same voice you order coffee.

Founder honesty · your strongest position
If you don't know an answer, say so. Yasser has built companies across 8 countries — he's seen every vendor pretend. A founder who is honest about what they've built and what they need help with is the most trusted person in the room. That's you.

About the platform Q1 – Q4

Q1.Is this real or a mockup?
“Every screen is real, running on real logic. The data is representative — not TLC's real participants — but the workflows and permissions are production-grade. You can click any screen right now.”
Q2.How is this different from Lumary / ShiftCare / Brevity / Careview?
“Those are point solutions — rostering, or compliance, or family portal. CareOS is one platform where a shift note becomes evidence, a goal becomes an outcome, a photo becomes a family moment. You'd need 5 of them stitched together to get what CareOS does natively.”
Q3.How does it handle NDIS compliance?
“Compliance is a by-product. Every shift note, med sign-off, and incident closure auto-files as evidence against the Practice Standards. The Compliance page shows 96% ready, live, right now.”
Q4.What about the new Support at Home / Aged Care reforms?
“The framework mapping is modular. When standards update, we map the new controls. The everyday care records don't change — every provider on the platform is instantly re-mapped.”

About TLC's specifics Q5 – Q8

Q5.We operate in 8 countries. Does this handle multiple jurisdictions?
“Yes. Compliance is framework-driven — NDIS is one framework, CQC in the UK is another, HIQA in Ireland is another. You configure per site. Same core, different overlays.”
Q6.We have 1,100+ staff. How does this scale?
“One truth, many views. Every worker sees only their people. Every team leader sees their team. Every executive sees the whole. Onboarding a worker takes minutes, not days.”
Q7.What's your pricing?
“Per-participant per-month, tiered by scale. Platform fee plus per-participant, with volume discount past 500. I'll send exact numbers after we understand your scope.”
Q8.Implementation timeline?
“Day one: executive dashboard live with your data. Week two: one care team piloted. Month one: family portal open to that team's families. Month three: full rollout. Role by role — not a 12-month migration.”

About the ask Q9 – Q11

Q9.What are you actually asking for?
“A partnership, not a licence sale. TLC as a design partner — you shape the roadmap, get preferential pricing, help us tune CareOS for enterprise-scale NDIS. In return, TLC becomes the reference case.”
Q10.Why me? Why now?
“Because you've built across 8 countries with 1,100+ staff. You've seen every category of software solve one thing badly. You know what a real solution looks like. And the sector's moving — providers who move first on unified platforms will define the next decade.”
Q11.What do you need from us to decide?
“A follow-up with your operations lead and your compliance lead — 45 minutes each, walking through their day. Then a written proposal specific to TLC. Two weeks turnaround.”

The last four — soft-ball ones Q12 – Q15

Q12.What happens if you go out of business?
“Every provider owns their data. Full export in standard formats, any time. We'd build data-escrow provisions into an enterprise contract so you get everything plus transition support.”
Q13.Do you use AI on our data?
“AI features are opt-in per feature. Nothing goes to third-party model providers without explicit config. Every AI output is human-reviewed before it becomes part of a record — AI drafts, humans approve. Happy to walk your IT lead through exactly which features exist.”
Q14.Have you had a security incident?
“None to disclose. Security-by-design — least privilege, encryption everywhere, audit logging on every path. Happy to walk your security team through our threat model in a follow-up.”
Q15.How do you handle families forwarding sensitive content?
“Watermarked exports with the viewer's identity. Sensitive fields can be view-only — no download. But no vendor can stop a camera pointed at a screen — the real security control is reducing conflict at the source. Parents who feel included don't screenshot to expose.”

Security · when you're not the expert One rule. One line. One escape hatch. That's all you need.

Rule #1 — Never fabricate a certification. If you claim SOC 2 or ISO 27001 and don't have them, you lose the deal in due diligence. Say “we're aligned, not certified — certification is on the roadmap.”
Rule #2 — “I'll bring our specialist in” is a strong answer, not a weak one. Enterprise sales works this way. Yasser will respect it.

The one line — memorise this word-for-word

Your security answer for anything you're not sure about
“I'm going to be honest with you — I'm not a security expert, and I won't pretend to be. I built CareOS from lived experience. Autism in my family, NDIS as a parent, business and customer experience as my background. That's the layer I've built.

Security is the next hire. If this partnership goes forward, I want to co-design the security architecture with your IT lead so it fits TLC specifically. That's exactly the kind of thing a design partner should shape — not something I hand you off-the-shelf.”

Short answers for the 5 you'll likely get

Where is participant data hosted?
“Cloud infrastructure with data residency configurable per client. For TLC, that means AU-sovereign hosting — participant data never leaves Australia. Each jurisdiction can have its own residency. Happy to walk your IT lead through the architecture.”
Is it encrypted?
“Yes. Encrypted in transit and at rest, industry standards. Every API call authenticated. Every data access logged.”
Who can see what?
“Role-based access. Eight roles, each sees only what their role needs. A support worker sees only their people. Every access is audit-logged. The family page shows how — three access tiers, per person.”
What if there's a breach?
“Standard notifiable data breach protocol — one hour internal, 24 hours to affected users and regulators per the OAIC scheme. Full incident playbook available in the procurement pack.”
Are you SOC 2 / ISO 27001 certified?
“We're aligned with the controls. Formal certification is on the roadmap — one of the reasons TLC as a design partner is powerful: certification would land before the first real participant record loads.”
Escape hatch · say this any time you feel stuck
“That's exactly the kind of question our security lead should walk your team through in a follow-up. I'd rather set that up properly than half-answer it now.”

What NOT to say tomorrow

Don't say specific numbers you can't back up
“TLS 1.3”, “AES-256”, “SOC 2 Type II” — if he asks a follow-up, you're stuck. Say “industry standards” and “aligned, not certified.”
Don't invent a CTO or a security team
Say “security is the next hire.” That's the truth. That's the deal you're proposing.
Don't dodge — redirect
Dodging feels like hiding. Redirecting to a follow-up feels like enterprise sales. Use the escape hatch.
