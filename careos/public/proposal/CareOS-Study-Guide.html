Chapter 1 · The story Everything starts with one source of truth.

The disability sector runs on 5–8 different systems that don't talk. Rostering here. Compliance there. Family portal is an afterthought. Notes live in a folder nobody opens. When somebody leaves, their knowledge walks out the door with them.

CareOS collapses all of that into one record per person, seen through role-shaped views. A parent sees wins. A worker sees today's plan. A CEO sees the whole organisation. Same data. Different lens. Every time.

Chapter 2 · For parents The emotional core — your lived experience, in software.

This is the section Yasser will feel the most. It's built from your own life. Nine features. Nine pains you've felt. Speak from that place.

Feature 01

KnockIn™ Arrivals — the second support begins, you know

You used to wait by the phone at 7:52am, wondering if support had shown up. Radio silence.
Workers tap arrival on entry (geofenced). Parents get an instant confirmation: “Leila arrived — 8:26am.” Every day, without asking.
“The moment support begins, the parent knows. Not a phone call. Not a follow-up. A notification, automatically, every day.”
Feature 02

Family Portal — wins, photos, moments as they happen

You asked your child questions they couldn't answer, and heard nothing from workers. Years of wondering how their day actually went.
Wins first, in plain language, updated through the day. Photos and moments as they happen — not a report at pickup, and never silence.
“The old model is: your child gets home, you ask them how it went, they can't tell you. New model: you already know. You saw the photo at 11am.”
Feature 03

Family Access — every parent has their own door

When parents live apart — or things aren't civil — one parent is always out of the loop. The child becomes the messenger.
Every parent has their own private login to the same truth. Nobody waits on anybody. Nothing filtered through anyone. Grandparents get view-only windows. All audit-logged, all consent-managed.
“Every family member has their own door to the same truth. No middleman. No child as messenger. That's a first in this sector.”
Feature 04

Timeline — nothing gets lost anymore

Your child's story used to live only in workers' heads — and walked out the door with every resignation.
Every shift, every win, every setback becomes part of one continuous story that belongs to the person. Years-long timeline that any authorised worker can pick up.
“Turnover in this sector is 30%+. When a worker leaves, their knowledge shouldn't leave with them. It belongs to the person they cared for.”
Feature 05

Budget Guardian — where did the plan money go?

Funds ran out mid-plan with no warning, and nobody could tell you where they went.
Same budget truth the provider sees — plain language, live, shortfalls flagged months early with a fix.
“Parents don't need dashboards. They need to know if the money's running out — and someone to tell them what to do about it.”
Feature 06

Review Ready — never walk in empty-handed

Funding got cut because you couldn't prove on paper what you live every single day.
1,240 evidence items auto-filed per plan. A language guard stops one careless sentence costing a family their support.
“Every parent going to a plan review is terrified they'll lose funding. This platform walks in with them.”
Feature 07

Learning Hub — answers at midnight

2am Googling, alone, hoping a stranger's forum post applied to your child.
Learning built around the specific diagnosis, communication style, and life stage — vetted answers from people who actually know.
Feature 08

Support Circle — you're not doing this alone

Every question felt like a battle you had to fight solo.
A circle of qualified people around the person, answering fast. Office and 000 always one tap away.
Feature 09 · The Promise

Three years from now — the compounding value

Most families end up with fragments — a few photos, half-remembered advice, restarted folders.
Every year this runs, it holds thousands more moments — what worked, what changed, who helped. The longer it runs, the better everyone knows the person. That's the promise.
“Software gets more valuable with time. Your child's care record should too. That's what compounding value looks like in disability support.”

Chapter 3 · For workers Their day, made easier — and their voice, heard.

The turnover crisis in this sector is real. CareOS gives workers a calmer day and a way for families' thank-yous to reach them. Both matter. Retention is the other half of quality care.

Feature 10

Today's Shift — the two-minute briefing

Workers walked in blind. First 15 minutes of every shift wasted catching up.
Goals, recent notes, red flags — prepared, not blind. Notes are spoken (dictated), not typed.
“A support worker's first two minutes shouldn't be reading a folder. It should be walking through the door already knowing.”
Feature 11

Smart Availability — say when you can work

Rosters made without asking. Workers pushed shifts they couldn't do. Constant back-and-forth.
Workers submit availability in the window; the AI fills open shifts while protecting permanent-to-participant pairings before flexing casuals.
“The AI never breaks a permanent placement to fill a hole. It protects continuity first, always.”
Feature 12

Time & Pay — get paid right, every time

Timesheet disputes. Missing hours. Awards misapplied.
Shifts become timesheets become pay runs — with the SCHADS award baked in. Every kilometre, every allowance, on the record.
Feature 13

Care DNA Matching — the right person, not just an available one

Rostering by who's free, not who's right. Bad matches burn workers and families out.
Care DNA profiles for participants (interests, communication, sensory needs) matched to worker skills, background, style. The right pairing, first time.

Chapter 5 · The AI principle Every AI feature serves one rule.

“Remove the thing the human shouldn't be doing. Not the human decision — the admin.” — The rule every AI feature in CareOS follows.

The AI in CareOS never makes a care decision. It fills spreadsheets after humans set preferences. It drafts a compliance notification after a human logs the incident. It transcribes a call so the coordinator can approve the follow-up. Humans decide. AI removes the typing.

★ Read this twice

Why this matters for enterprise NDIS. Every provider is nervous about AI in vulnerable-person contexts. CareOS's line is clear and defensible: AI drafts, humans approve. Every AI output is human-reviewed before it becomes part of a record. There is no autonomous AI in the care loop. That's a bright line — and it's the right one.

Chapter 6 · The moat Why nobody else can copy this quickly.

Yasser will ask: “what's stopping [any competitor] from building this?” Here's the honest answer.

1. Lived experience
The tour is built from real family pain. Nobody at Lumary, Brevity, or ShiftCare has felt the 2am Google spiral. That authenticity shows in every screen.
2. Architectural bet
Every competitor is bolted-together modules. CareOS was built on one source of truth from day one. Rebuilding on that foundation costs years of engineering.
3. Family-first design
Sector software treats families as an afterthought. CareOS treats them as first-class users with their own doors. Retrofitting that is culture, not code.
4. AI principle discipline
“AI drafts, humans approve.” Every feature. Every time. Competitors will chase autonomous AI and get burned. CareOS won't.
5. Breadth × depth
26 modules, all real, all integrated. Nobody adds 26 modules in 12 months. And nobody rebuilds them into one platform in less than three years.
6. The compounding record
Every year of care makes the record more valuable. Providers who start now win in three years. Providers who switch later never catch up.
