CAT. 05 — ABOUT US

From a backyard swing-set to Toyota specialists.

Corolla Spares was founded from humble beginnings — operating at hobby level out of the backyard of the parents’ place, using our pre-loved childhood swing-set as a makeshift hoist. We found an opportunity to specialise in this niche, supplying new and used parts for the Toyota enthusiast community.

Is it only Corollas? Not any more. We now supply parts for MR2, Celica, Corona and more — and we love anything with a 4AGE, working to be the dominant parts provider for the mighty Twincam. Based in Sydney, operating online, and always up for a chat — send us a message for any questions or advice, and follow the never-ending project cars on our socials.
