What we do

One workshop. The whole car.

Brakes are the name on the sign. The bay handles everything else your car needs to pass, run and stay on the road.

Better Brakes servicing

Pads, disc rotors, fluid, handbrake, European and everyday cars alike. Every brake job starts with a four-wheel inspection and a written report, and the inspection fee is waived when you go ahead with the work.

Mechanical servicing

Log book servicing for new cars, minor and major services, oil changes, filters, cooling systems and health checks. Stamped to keep your warranty intact.

Auto electrical & batteries

Batteries tested and replaced, charging and starting faults traced, lights and wiring sorted. The warning light gets diagnosed, not guessed at.

Tyres

Pirelli, Bridgestone and Goodride on the shelf, fitted and balanced while you wait. Puncture repairs and rotations too.

Air conditioning

Rego & reports

Brake inspection & report

All four wheels, written up and road-tested.

  • Brake pad and disc rotor condition on all four wheels
  • Visual check for fluid leaks
  • Brake fluid condition tested (replace every 2 years)
  • Brakes and handbrake adjusted
  • Road test
Fee waived with brake work
