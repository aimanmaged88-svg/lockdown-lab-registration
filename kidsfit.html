Session 1

20-minute session. No equipment. Boxed text = say it out loud. Grey italics = what you do. Hit Start on the session timer as you begin and it will tell you when to move on.

Golden rule: keep them moving. Don't spend minutes explaining a game. Demonstrate while you speak and start quickly.

Welcome

0:00 to 1:30

Bring the kids together, standing, with space around each of them.

“Good morning, everyone! Welcome to the very first session of Get the Kids Fit! For the next month, we're going to start our mornings doing something really simple… MOVING!”
“You don't have to be good at sport. You don't have to be the fastest. You don't have to be the strongest. And you definitely don't have to be the best at anything. We're just here to have fun.”
“We're going to play games, go on adventures, try different movements and probably be a little bit silly sometimes! There is really only one thing I want from you. Have fun and keep moving.”
“If you don't like one way of moving, that's okay. Find another way that works for you. There are no winners or losers here. We're doing this together. Okay… Who is ready for our FIRST Get the Kids Fit session?”

Let them respond.

“I can't hear you! WHO'S READY?”

Let them get louder.

“Okay! It is time to MOVE!”

Moving Warm-up

1:30 to 5:00

Kids spread out so they can move safely. You demonstrate everything while you talk. Keep it flowing, barely any stopping between moves.

1
Reach up
“Everyone reach up as HIGH as you can! Right up to the sky! Can you make yourself even taller?”

Hold briefly. “And relax.”

2
Side reach
“Reach over to one side… And now the other. Side… And side.”

Repeat a few times.

3
Shoulder rolls
“Let's wake up those shoulders. Big circles backwards. And now forwards.”
4
Gentle body turns
“Now turn gently from side to side. Keep those feet comfortable. Side… And side.”
5
March
“Okay, let's get those feet moving. Start marching! Nice and easy.”

After a few seconds: “Can we make that march a little bit quicker?”

6
Knees up
“Now bring those knees a little higher. You choose how high. Keep moving!”
7
Side steps
“Now side to side. Step… Together… Step… Together.”

Pick up the pace a little.

8
Heel kicks
“Let's get those feet moving a little faster. Bring your heels back towards you. Keep going!”
9
High and low
“Now listen carefully! When I say HIGH, reach HIGH! When I say LOW, get LOW! Ready? HIGH! LOW! HIGH! LOW! HIGH! LOW!”

Speed the calls up gradually.

10
Shake it out
“And SHAKE everything out! Shake your arms! Shake your legs! Shake your whole body!”
Transition, keep them moving“Okay! We're warmed up. And now we're getting started. Remember… KEEP MOVING AND HAVE FUN!”

Activity 1
Traffic Lights

5:00 to 9:00

Kids spread through the whole space.

“Everyone start moving around the space. Walk, jog or move however feels good. But listen carefully because I'm controlling the traffic lights! When I say…”
GREEN = GO!AMBER = SLOW MOTION!RED = FREEZE!
“Let's try it. GREEN!”

Let them move.

“RED!”

Pause.

GREEN!AMBER!RED!

Keep switching commands for about a minute.

After about one minute, add colours

“Okay… That was WAY too easy. We're adding some new colours.”
BLUE = reach up HIGH!PURPLE = turn around!ORANGE = SILLY WALK!

Demonstrate something ridiculous for the silly walk.

“Ready? GREEN!”

Call colours faster and faster. Keep the energy high.

RED!PURPLE!GREEN!AMBER!BLUE!ORANGE!GREEN!
If someone does the wrong action: “Oh no! Wrong colour! Doesn't matter. KEEP MOVING!”
Transition, finish on green“GREEN! And DON'T STOP! Because we're going straight into our next game!”

Activity 2
The Floor is Lava

9:00 to 14:00
Before you start, quickly point out a few safe areas. Outdoors: two opposite ends of the field. Indoors: same thing, or use lines on the floor.
  • Corners
  • Lines on the floor
  • Walls
  • Imaginary islands you name
“Keep moving! But I need to warn you about something… At any moment… The floor could turn into… LAVA! When I shout THE FLOOR IS LAVA! you've got FIVE seconds to reach one of our safe areas. Ready? Keep moving…”

Wait a few seconds.

“THE FLOOR IS LAVA! FIVE! FOUR! THREE! TWO! ONE! SAFE!”

Straight away:

“Okay, the lava's gone. Back out!”

They start moving again.

Round 2

“Now we're moving with GIANT STEPS!”

Let them move, then:

“THE FLOOR IS LAVA! FIVE! FOUR! THREE! TWO! ONE! SAFE!”
“Back out! This time… TINY STEPS!”

Round 3

“Now SIDEWAYS!”
“THE FLOOR IS LAVA! FIVE! FOUR! THREE! TWO! ONE! SAFE!”

Round 4

“Uh-oh… The ground has turned into really sticky MUD! Lift those feet!”

Then suddenly:

“THE FLOOR IS LAVA!”

Keep going for several rounds until the timer says move on. Mix the movement each time: hopping, backwards, tip-toes, animal walks.

Nobody is out for missing a safe zone. Just say: “Quick! Quick! Quick! SAFE! And we're back!”
Transition“Keep moving! Something else is happening now…”

Cool Down

14:00 to 17:00

Wherever you are in the game when the timer says move on, go straight into the cool down. Slow your own voice and pace right down.

“WOW! Amazing work. Now keep moving… But let's bring everything right down.”
1
Slow walk
“Start walking slowly around the space. Nice and easy.” “Take a big breath IN…”

Breathe in with them.

“And OUT…”

Repeat.

2
Gentle march
“Now a really gentle march on the spot. Let your breathing slow down.”
3
Reach up
“Reach both arms up nice and tall. Big breath IN…” “And breathe OUT.”
4
Side stretch
“Reach over to one side… Hold… And back. Other side… Hold… And back.”
5
Shoulders
“Roll those shoulders backwards. Nice and slowly.” “And forwards.”
6
Shake out
“Shake your arms out. Shake your legs. Let everything relax.”
7
Final breaths
“Stand nice and comfortably. Big breath IN…”

Raise arms.

“And OUT…”

Lower arms. Again:

“IN…” “And OUT.” “One last one… IN… And OUT.”

Day One
Pep Talk

17:00 to 20:00

Bring the kids together.

“Guys… YOU WERE AMAZING! That was our very first Get the Kids Fit session. And look what you just did. You moved. You played. You laughed. You tried different things. And we did it TOGETHER.”
“Nobody needed to be the fastest. Nobody needed to be the strongest. Nobody needed to be brilliant at sport. You just needed to get involved and MOVE. And you did exactly that.”

Pause.

“Now… We've got a challenge for you. We're coming back TOMORROW! Who's coming back?”

Encourage hands and responses.

“Who thinks they can do it again tomorrow?”

Respond enthusiastically.

“YES!” “And here's the BIG challenge… Who thinks they can keep coming back and do EVERY Get the Kids Fit SESSION THIS MONTH?”

Encourage hands.

“Look at all those hands! We think you can do it too. And every time you come back, we're going to try something different. More games. More challenges. More adventures. And a LOT more moving.”
“Give yourselves a MASSIVE clap!”

Lead the applause.

“Day One… DONE! You guys were amazing. We can't wait to see you tomorrow! Well done everyone!”
Hand back“Now I'm going to hand back over to your teachers for the next instructions! See you tomorrow!!”
