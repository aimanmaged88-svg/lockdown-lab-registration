## Notes that matter

**Say "instant download" everywhere.** It's the whole advantage over physical
sellers once October is half gone — no shipping cut-off, no disappointment.

**The IP line is deliberate.** "All artwork original. Not from any film or
franchise" on every listing. It's true, it heads off a buyer expecting licensed
merch, and it's the sentence you want on the record if anyone ever asks.

**Pin the same product more than once.** Each product above has one pin written
for it; there are twelve finished pin images in `pins/`. Pinterest rewards
fresh images over repeat links, so keep making new pins for the same product
rather than re-pinning the same image.

**Don't put a film title in a listing title or a tag.** Not even "inspired by".
Board descriptions and blog text are commentary and are a different matter —
the listing is commerce, and that's where trademark use gets you removed.
