The read

Three things decide this before you build anything

On Scream

There is no new Scream this season — Scream 8 is tracking for 2027 (a Paramount horror slot sits on 16 July 2027). But the parody Scary Movie already ran this year to roughly $231M, and Ghostface stays a permanent top-tier costume search. So Scream is an affiliate line, never a product line. Same for every mask you were picturing.

On timing

It is 18 September. A physical product listed after about 10 October cannot reliably land before the 31st, and print-on-demand fulfilment slows as the queue fills. Digital — printables, party packs, cut files, poster art, wallpapers — launches today and sells at full margin until Halloween night. Digital first, physical only if it is listed this fortnight.

On the shops

You cannot sell a film's name, characters, masks or artwork. What you can sell is the look each film pushes into search: witch-house autumn, blood-red prom, haunted carnival, camp slasher, dark-academia thriller. The film is the traffic wave. The aesthetic is the shelf. Pinterest's own data has vintage Halloween up about 200% year on year — that demand does not belong to any studio.

The rules

What you can and can't put on a product

This is the part that ends shops. Studios file takedowns through October — Etsy and Pinterest action them automatically, and repeat strikes close accounts. The line is simple and it is not about how much you change the artwork.

Yours to sell

  • Aesthetics, palettes, motifs — witch kitchen, prom red, haunted carnival, camp horror
  • Generic archetypes: final girl, camp counsellor, fortune teller, the babysitter
  • Public-domain sources — Victorian ephemera, Poe, Dickens, pre-1930 illustration
  • Your own type, your own camp names, your own slogans
  • Film titles as commentary: in a board description or a blog post, never on a product or in a listing title

Not yours, at any price

  • Titles and character names — Scream, Ghostface, Jason, Carrie, Camp Crystal Lake, Resident Evil
  • Masks, logos, taglines, poster recreations, redrawn characters
  • "Inspired by" in a listing title — that is still a trademark use
  • Costume replicas of a specific film look
  • Cloaked or shortened affiliate links — Pinterest flags them as spam and blocks accounts. Paste the full URL

The licensed merchandise is still earnable: affiliate it. Link the official mask from a retailer, disclose it ("affiliate link" near the top of the description, per Pinterest and FTC rules), take the commission. It is the only legal way to monetise a search for a film's name — and it needs no stock, no shipping and no cutoff date.

The money

Where the store lives, and what a sale actually pays

One decision to make today. A true Pinterest shop — the Shop tab, a product catalogue, shopping ads — needs a claimed website with a product feed, which means Shopify or WooCommerce. Etsy can't feed a Pinterest catalogue, but it lists digital products in minutes, delivers them itself and brings its own search traffic.

The call: open on Etsy today and pin manually, then build the Shopify catalogue for Christmas. The Halloween window is too short to spend on store plumbing; the Christmas window is long enough to deserve it.
