Questions

Before you buy

What actually turns up?

A zip holding two PDFs — one A4, one US Letter — and a short printing and licence note. Wallpapers arrive as JPEGs in two phone sizes. No physical items, nothing in the post.

Will it eat my ink?

No. Every printable sheet is line work and type on white, the way a letterpress broadside actually was. There are no full-bleed dark backgrounds anywhere in the printables — that is a deliberate design decision, not a shortcut. The wallpapers are dark, but they only ever touch a screen.

Can I use these for my shop, my event, my client?

Print as much as you like for yourself, your home, your party or your classroom. You may not resell the files, share them, sell printed copies or fold them into another product. If you want to use them commercially, ask — we will usually say yes.

My printer cuts off the edges.

Everything sits inside a 12mm safe margin, so a normal home printer will not clip it — as long as you print at 100% and not “fit to page”, which shrinks the sheet and throws the cut lines out.

Is any of this from a film or a TV show?

None of it. Every act, label, name and line here was written and drawn for this shop. Nothing is borrowed from anyone's film, book or franchise, and nothing carries anyone else's trademark.

Refunds?

Digital files cannot really be returned, so we do not offer refunds as a rule — but if something is broken, wrong, or not what the listing promised, reply to your order and we will fix it or refund you.
