# Community guidelines

UNC Thoughts is for **useful conversations**, not empty engagement. These are the
rules for the adults-only (18+) community.

## The spirit

- **Useful over loud.** We reward help, not popularity — that's why reactions are
  "useful", not likes.
- **Direct, honest, supportive.** Like advice from a good uncle. No preaching, no
  trying too hard to be inspirational.
- **All levels welcome.** Hype people up. Never tear anyone down.

## The rules

1. Be respectful. No harassment, hate, or personal attacks.
2. Keep it useful and on-topic for the space you're in.
3. No spam, self-promotion, scams, or off-platform selling.
4. Protect privacy — yours and others'. Don't share private contact details or
   anyone's location.
5. **Nothing involving minors.** This community is adults 18+ only. Content *for*
   young players is shared publicly through parents, guardians and coaches.
6. Nutrition talk is **general education**, never individual medical advice, and
   never diet/weight-loss/body-shame content. See a professional for personal
   questions.
7. Get consent before posting identifiable people, especially in clips.
8. Follow moderator decisions. Enforcement reasons are shown transparently.

## Reporting & enforcement

- Anyone can **report** a post. Reports go to the moderation queue.
- Moderators can hide/remove posts, lock discussions, enable slow mode, and
  restrict announcements — always with a recorded, transparent reason.
- Actions are logged in the audit trail. Repeated or serious breaches lead to
  removal.

## Contributors & experts

Verified expert status is **granted manually** and records the basis for
verification. Contributors get a badge, not special latitude to break the rules.
