Straight talk

Three things you need to know before you spend a dollar

1. You cannot re-upload this video. It's @HEMZZ.6's face and footage. Re-posting it gets the account killed and gets you rejected from every affiliate program you apply to. You copy the formula, film your own. That part is completely fair game — nobody owns a script structure.
2. TikTok Shop does not exist in Australia. The thing you're picturing — tag a product in the video, viewer taps, you get paid — isn't available here, and TikTok has said there's no confirmed local rollout. So the AU route is bio-link affiliate: Amazon Associates AU, or brand programs through Commission Factory.
3. Affiliate maths on a $25 scrubber is brutal. AU beauty programs pay around 5% on an ~$80 basket — roughly $4 a sale. A million dollars of commission at $4 a sale is 250,000 sales. That's not a content problem, it's an arithmetic problem. The Money tab shows you the real curve.
So here's the actual play. Affiliate is not the business — it's the instrument. You post the content, watch which product genuinely makes strangers pull out a card, and let the affiliate links tell you for free which one wins. Then you white-label the winner. A silicone scrubber lands at roughly $1–3 a unit; the same video that earned you $4 in commission now earns you $20+ in margin, and you own the customer instead of renting them. Same face, same script, same 41 seconds — five times the money. Run affiliate for 90 days to find the winner, then flip.
