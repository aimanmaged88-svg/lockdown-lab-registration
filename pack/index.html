The honest bit

Do our runners have to download anything?

No. It's a link. Their phone can add it to the home screen so it opens like a normal app, but there's no app store and nothing to install.

Do they need accounts?

No passwords, no emails, no sign-up. A name and the club's code. That's the whole door — which is also why there's nothing to reset when someone forgets something.

What if they already use Strava?

Good, keep it. We deliberately don't collect distance, pace or times — you'd only be entering the same run twice. Runners can put their Strava on their card so the club can find them on it.

So what do you actually measure?

Turning up. Check-ins on club runs, and how many weeks in a row you've made one. It's the one number a run club actually cares about and the one no tracker owns.

Is our chat private?

Only people with your club's code can get in, and clubs can't see each other at all. If the code gets out, the captain rolls it and the old one stops working.

Can we leave?

Monthly, whenever. Ask and we'll hand you your club's data. Stop paying and you drop back to Free rather than getting locked out.
