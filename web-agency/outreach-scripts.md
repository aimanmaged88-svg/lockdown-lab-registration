# Outreach Scripts — copy, paste, tweak, send

The whole business lives here. Sending these is the one thing only you can do. Aim for **10–20 messages a week**. Keep them short and human — no essays.

Replace `[...]` bits. Drop in your links: your site + the 3 demos.

---

## 1. Warm intro (people you already know) — SEND THESE FIRST

> Hey [Name]! Quick one — I've started building websites for local businesses. Simple, sharp ones that actually bring in customers, live within a week. Here's some of my work: [your-site-link]
>
> Do you (or anyone you know) need a website, or have one that's looking a bit tired? Happy to sort it out. 🙌

**Follow-up if no reply (3–4 days later):**
> No worries if not! Just let me know if anyone comes to mind — a good site pays for itself pretty quick. 😊

---

## 2. The free-demo move (cold local businesses) — HIGHEST CONVERSION

**Step 1:** Find a local business with no site or a bad one (Google "[suburb] [trade]"). Spend 30 min making them a sample homepage — copy one of your demos, swap the name/colours/photos.

**Step 2:** Message them (DM, email, or in person):
> Hi [Business], I'm [Your Name] — I build websites for local businesses here in [area]. I actually put together a quick sample homepage for [Business] to show what it could look like — no charge, no obligation. Want me to send it over?

**Step 3:** When they say yes, send the link:
> Here you go: [demo-link] 😃 This is just a rough first version. If you like the direction, I can have the full site live within 5 days — it's $749 to build plus $79/month to host it, keep it updated and handle all the tech (no lock-in). Want to jump on a quick 15-min call?

---

## 3. Cold DM / email (Instagram, no demo)

> Hi [Business]! Love what you do 🙌 I noticed you [don't have a website yet / your site could use a refresh]. I build clean, mobile-friendly sites for local businesses — live in 5 days, from $749. Here's some recent work: [your-site-link]. Keen to have a quick chat?

---

## 4. In-person (walking into a shop/café/gym)

> "Hey, I'm [Name] — I actually build websites for local businesses around here. I had a look and reckon I could make you a really sharp one. Mind if I send you a couple of examples?"

Then get their number/IG and follow up with script #2 or #3.

---

## 5. The "founding client" discount (to land your first 1–2)

> Since I'm just launching, I'm doing my first couple of sites at a **founding rate — $499 to build** (normally $749) in exchange for a testimonial once you're happy. Same site, same care. Want in?

Use this ONLY for your first 1–2 clients. After that, full price.

---

## 6. Asking for the testimonial (do this every time a client is happy)

> So glad you love it! 🙌 Would you mind writing me a quick sentence or two I can put on my site? Even just what it was like working with me and how the site's going. Means the world when you're starting out. 🙏

Also ask: *"Know anyone else who could use one?"* — referrals are your best channel once you're rolling.

---

## 7. Upsell (to existing clients, later)

> Hey [Name]! Your site's been running great. I've just started offering [online booking / a second page / Google Business setup] — reckon it'd get you even more enquiries. Want me to add it on?

---

## Quick tips

- **Speed wins.** Reply to interested people within an hour if you can. Leads go cold fast.
- **One clear ask per message.** Usually: "want to see it?" or "quick call?"
- **Don't argue price.** If they hesitate, point at the care plan value + "no lock-in, cancel anytime."
- **Track it.** Keep a simple note: who you messaged, when, and the reply. Follow up once. That's where the money hides.
