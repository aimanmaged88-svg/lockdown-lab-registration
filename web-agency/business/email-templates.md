# SiteFive — Professional email templates

Warm but professional. These are for email (and polished DMs) once a lead is talking to you — more formal than the cold Instagram scripts. Replace `[brackets]`. Paste your signature (bottom) on every one.

---

## 1. Reply to a new enquiry (from the site form or a DM)
**Subject:** Your website with SiteFive 👋

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for reaching out — great to hear from you! I'd love to help get [Business] a website that brings in more customers.
>
> The quickest next step is a free 15-minute call so I can understand your business and show you exactly what I'd build. No pressure, no jargon.
>
> Are you free [suggest 2 times, e.g. "tomorrow morning or Thursday afternoon"]? Or grab a time here: [your Calendly link].
>
> In the meantime, here's some of my recent work: [your site link]
>
> Talk soon,
> [Your Name]

## 2. After the call — send the proposal
**Subject:** Your SiteFive proposal for [Business]

> Hi [Name],
>
> Great chatting today! As promised, here's your proposal — everything we discussed, the price options, and my guarantees all on one page: [attach proposal-template.html as a PDF].
>
> Quick recap:
> • A [X]-page website, live within **5 days** of you sending your details
> • **Live in 5 days or the build is free**, you approve the design before you pay, and no lock-in
> • Two ways to pay: $749 build + $79/mo, or $0 upfront at $119/mo
>
> If you're happy, just reply **"Yes, let's do it"** and I'll send the quick starter form and get straight into it.
>
> Any questions at all, just ask.
>
> Cheers,
> [Your Name]

## 3. They said yes — onboarding
**Subject:** Let's build your website 🚀

> Hi [Name],
>
> Brilliant — excited to build this for you! Two quick things to get started:
>
> **1. The details.** Fill in this short form (5 mins) and send me your logo + any photos: [link to intake form]
> **2. The deposit.** To lock in your build, here's the invoice for the 50% deposit ([$375]): [attach invoice]. Bank details are on it.
>
> The moment both are in, your 5-day clock starts and I'll have a first version back to you fast.
>
> Can't wait to get going,
> [Your Name]

>

## 5. Delivering the site / go-live
**Subject:** 🎉 Your website is ready

> Hi [Name],
>
> Your site is live: **[link]** 🎉 Take a look on your phone and computer.
>
> I've also set up [your business email / Google listing]. If you'd like any small tweaks — a wording change, a different photo — just reply and I'll sort it (that's included in your plan).
>
> Really happy with how it turned out. Congratulations on being properly online!
>
> [Your Name]

## 6. Asking for a testimonial (a few days after go-live)
**Subject:** Quick favour? 🙏

> Hi [Name],
>
> So glad you're happy with the site! When you have a sec, would you mind sending me a sentence or two about your experience — what it was like working together and how the site's going? It genuinely helps me a lot.
>
> And if you know any other local business owners who could use a website, I'd be grateful for the introduction.
>
> Thanks so much,
> [Your Name]
