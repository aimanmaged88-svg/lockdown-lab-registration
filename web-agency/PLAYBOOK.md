## The offer (memorise this)

> **"A simple, beautiful website for your business, live in 5 days. $799 to build it, $79/month to keep it looked after. No lock-in."**

Two revenue streams:
1. **Build fee** — $799 once-off (your quick cash).
2. **Care plan** — $79/month recurring (the real business — this is what stacks).

**Do the maths:** 20 clients on care plans = **$1,580/month recurring**, before you build a single new site. That's the goal. Chase the monthly plans on every single client.

## Phase 1 — Get set up (this week, ~2 hours)

- [ ] **Deploy your website.** It's built. See "Deploying" below — free, 5 minutes.
- [ ] **Get a business email.** `hello@yourname.com.au` looks 10x more legit than gmail. (Google Workspace ~$8/mo, or free with most domain purchases.)
- [ ] **Set up a way to get paid.** Simplest: send an invoice, take bank transfer. Better: Stripe payment links (free to set up, ~1.7% fee). Start simple.
- [ ] **Save the 3 demo links + your site link** in your phone notes. These are your ammo.
- [ ] **Pick your first niche.** You already know fitness/coaching — start there. It makes outreach 10x easier.

## Phase 2 — Land your first client (weeks 1–3)

**The rule: you go get clients, they don't come to you. Do outreach every week, no exceptions.**

Best channels, in order of what works:

1. **People you already know (warmest — start here).**
   Message 20 people: gym owners, PTs, café owners, tradies, parents at your sessions who run a business. Use the "warm intro" script in `outreach-scripts.md`.

2. **The free-demo move (highest conversion).**
   Find a local business with a bad or no website. Build them a quick homepage using one of your demos as a base (swap the name/colours — takes 30 min). Message them: *"Made you a sample homepage — want to see it?"* This works because you've already done the work. Script is in the outreach file.

3. **Instagram / local Facebook groups.**
   You run @lockdownlablive — you know IG. Post your demos. "Local business? I build websites — DM me."

**Goal of Phase 2: ONE paying client.** Even at a discount ($499 to get the first testimonial is fine). One real client = proof + testimonial + confidence. That's worth more than the money.

## Phase 3 — Turn 1 into a machine (month 2)

- [ ] Get a **testimonial + before/after screenshots** the moment you finish. Ask for it directly.
- [ ] Put the real client on your site's "Our work" section (replace a demo).
- [ ] **Raise your price** for client #2. Then again for #3.
- [ ] Use the **intake form** every time so you're not chasing people for content — that's the #1 time-killer.
- [ ] Push the **care plan** on every client. This is where the business really lives.

## Phase 4 — Scale (month 3+)

- [ ] Stack care plans. Aim for 20 clients on $79/mo = $1,580/mo recurring.
- [ ] Add upsells: extra pages, online booking, Google Business setup, "we post for you" content, basic SEO.
- [ ] When you're too busy building: raise prices (fewer clients, more each) OR hire a designer/VA and become the person who sells + manages.

## The 3 rules that make or break it

1. **You must sell, not just build.** Building is your strength — outreach will feel awkward. The winners do it every week anyway. 30 minutes of messages > 3 hours of tweaking a design.
2. **Always take a 50% deposit up front.** Never build for a real client for free.
3. **Recurring beats one-off.** Chase the $79/month. That's what turns "I make websites" into "I own a company."

## Deploying your website (free, 5 minutes)

Your site is plain HTML files — they host anywhere for free. Easiest option, **Netlify Drop**:

1. Go to **app.netlify.com/drop**
2. Drag the whole `web-agency` folder onto the page.
3. Done — you get a free live link like `fiveday.netlify.app`.
4. Later: buy a domain (~$15/yr from Netlify or GoDaddy — e.g. `fiveday.com.au`) and point it at the site. Netlify walks you through it.

The demo links inside your site already point to `demos/...`, so they'll work automatically once the folder is deployed.

> Alternative: since you already deploy the Lockdown Lab to Netlify, you can add this as a second site on the same account.
