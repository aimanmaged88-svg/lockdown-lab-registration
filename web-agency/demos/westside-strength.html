Good to know

Questions, answered

I'm a total beginner — is this for me?
Absolutely. Most of our members started with zero gym experience. Your first session is a free chat and a gentle assessment — no judgement, no pressure. We meet you exactly where you are and build from there.
Do I need to sign a long contract?
Never. Everything is month-to-month or pay-as-you-go. We want you to stay because it's working, not because you're locked in. Pause or cancel any time.
What should I bring to my first session?
Just comfortable clothes, a water bottle and a good attitude. We've got all the equipment. Come 5 minutes early so we can show you around the studio.
Where exactly are you located?
We're in the heart of Marrickville, a short walk from the station with street parking nearby. We'll send you the exact address and directions when you book your free session.
