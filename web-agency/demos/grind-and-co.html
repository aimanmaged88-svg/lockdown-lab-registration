Our coffee

Small batch, big on care.

We source a rotating single-origin from growers we actually know, roast it in small batches every week, and dial in every shot on our La Marzocco. No burnt bitterness, no rush — just balanced, honest coffee poured by people who love it. Grab it to go, or settle in with a book and let the morning take its time.
