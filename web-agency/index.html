Questions

Good to know

Do I really only need to spend 30 minutes?

Pretty much. A 15-minute call and one short form is all we need from you. We write the words, design the layout, and handle every technical bit. You just review and approve.

What if I don't have a logo or photos?

No problem. We can design a clean text-based logo and use professional stock imagery that fits your business, at no extra cost.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The site is yours. The care plan covers hosting, updates and support — but you're never locked in, and you can cancel anytime.

What does the $79/month actually cover?

Hosting, your domain, business email, security, backups, and unlimited small changes — swap a price, add a photo, update your hours — just message us. It's the "set and forget" part.

Can you do more than a landing page?

Absolutely. Multi-page sites, online booking, simple online stores and more. The 5-day landing page is our starting point — we scope bigger projects on the call.

How do I pay?

A 50% deposit to start, the rest when your site goes live. The monthly care plan begins once you're live. Bank transfer or card.
