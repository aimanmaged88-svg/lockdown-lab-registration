# 🚀 LAUNCH TONIGHT — your 60-minute checklist

Everything's built. This is the exact order to go live tonight. ~1 hour, mostly waiting.

---

## ✅ Step 1 — Put your website live (10 min, free)

1. On your computer, go to **app.netlify.com/drop**
2. Drag the whole **`web-agency`** folder onto the page.
3. Wait ~30 seconds → you get a live link like `sitefive.netlify.app`.
4. Click it, check it works, click the 3 demos. **You're live.** 🎉
5. (Optional, do later) Buy `sitefive.com.au` (~$15/yr) and connect it — Netlify walks you through it.

> Tip: rename the Netlify site to `sitefive` in Site settings so the link reads `sitefive.netlify.app`.

## ✅ Step 2 — Set up your Instagram (15 min)

1. Make a new Instagram account (or convert one): handle **@sitefive.co** (or `@sitefive.sydney` / `@getsitefive` if taken).
2. Switch to a **Business/Professional account** (Settings → Account type). Free, and unlocks contact buttons + insights.
3. Set the profile pic to your logo — use **`brand/ig-profile.png`** (your finished SiteFive icon). Done.
4. Paste this bio:
   > 🌐 Websites for local business
   > ⚡ Live in 5 days · from $799
   > 📍 Sydney · DM us "SITE" to start 👇
5. Add your website link (your new Netlify/`.com.au` link).
6. Post **Post #1** (`instagram/posts/01-launch.jpg`) with its caption from `instagram/captions.md`.
7. Put it on your Story too, with the link sticker.

## ✅ Step 3 — Send your first 10 messages (20 min) — THIS is the business

Open `outreach-scripts.md`. Use the **warm-intro script**. Message **10 people you already know** who run a business or know people who do — gym owners, PTs, café/shop owners, tradies, parents from your sessions.

Paste, tweak the name, send. Include your new website link. That's it.

> 10 messages tonight is realistically 1–3 replies, and your first client is usually hiding in your existing network. Don't skip this step — the site doesn't make money, the messages do.

## ✅ Step 4 — Be ready to say yes (5 min)

- Decide your bank details / how you'll take the 50% deposit.
- Keep `client-contract.md` and `intake-form.md` handy — when someone says yes, you send the contract, take the deposit, send the intake form, and you're building.

---

## What to do this week
- Post the next Instagram image every 1–2 days (captions all written in `instagram/captions.md`).
- Send **10 more messages every day**. Follow up once with anyone who didn't reply.
- Try the **free-demo move** (outreach script #2) on 2–3 local businesses with bad/no sites — highest conversion there is.
- First client? Do a great job, get a testimonial + before/after, put them on your site.

## The one rule
The website and the posts are your shopfront. **The messages are the business.** Do the outreach every day and this works. 🙌
